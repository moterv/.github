# Moterv

Moterv develops performance chemistry and the software that connects products, workshops, distributors and teams. Moterv Automotive is our first commercial division; Moterv Robotics is an R&D direction.

Our product and platform monorepo is private while the team develops and evaluates its services. For project or security enquiries, contact a Moterv organisation owner through GitHub.
